We partner with hotel owners, investors, developers, and restaurateurs to design, develop, launch, operate, and optimize hospitality businesses across East Africa.

From concept creation and pre-opening to operational management and commercial growth, we deliver practical solutions that create exceptional guest experiences and sustainable business performance.

Resort

Masheda Palms Resort

Kampala Uganda

Masheda Palms Resort is a premium asset within the Sinel Hospitality portfolio, offering a high-yield blend of eco-luxury and strategic location. Managed end-to-end—from initial market positioning to active day-to-day operations—this 20-acre boutique resort is located in Buyala Town, Kisamula, just off Mityana Road. Situated a convenient 45-minute drive from Kampala, the property is optimized for high-occupancy weekend getaways, family retreats, and corporate events. The development seamlessly integrates traditional, grass-thatched architecture with modern luxury amenities, driving strong brand equity and robust unit-level performance.

Ituri, DRC

Hotel BiaExauce is a strategic hospitality asset managed end-to-end by Sinel Hospitality, driving operational excellence from its initial market positioning through to daily unit-level execution. Kampala Uganda

Managed end-to-end by Sinel Hospitality—from initial concept development through to daily unit-level execution—the venue is highly regarded for its sophisticated decor, inviting atmosphere, and exceptional guest relations. Robyson Residency Kinshasa

Kinshasa, DRC

Robyson Residency is a premium hospitality asset managed end-to-end by Sinel Hospitality—from strategic market analysis and initial concept deployment through to daily unit-level execution.
